🌼ABOUT US


Poovarasankulam is a small village in the Northern Province
of Sri Lanka, characterized by its serene rural landscape
and close-knit community. Agriculture forms the backbone of
local life, with paddy fields and small-scale farming shaping
the daily routines of residents. Despite past challenges due to
regional conflicts, the village reflects resilience and cultural
richness, with traditional customs, festivals, and religious
practices continuing to be integral to social life.
